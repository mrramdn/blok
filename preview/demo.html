Questions

Can I bring my own paper?

Yes, up to A3 and 300 gsm. Coated paper does not take riso ink well; the workshop hosts will tell you what works.

How do I get a table at the market?

All 64 tables are taken for this edition. Join the newsletter to hear when applications open for 2027.

Can I get a refund?

Until 13 November. After that you can pass your ticket on: change the name in the email you received.

Passes cost more at the door

A day pass is Rp55.000 at the door, cash only. Online it is Rp45.000 and skips the queue.
